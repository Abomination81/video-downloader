# Video controls that stay out of the way.

**Abomination81 Toolkit v0.13.5** · Built by [abomination81](https://abomination81.com) · [X @abomination81](https://x.com/Abomination81)

The floating seek palette is now a slim control bar along the bottom of the video. Hover to seek, play or pause, adjust volume or speed, pop out the video, or enter fullscreen. Leave the video and the bar disappears immediately.

The fallback appears only when a video lacks usable controls. Native controls and recognized custom players—including controls that fade in on hover—keep their own interface. Embedded players, shadow-root players and transparent overlays are covered, while keyboard shortcuts and remembered playback speed remain available.

Existing unified installations receive this signed update automatically. No new permissions or reinstall.
