# Abomination81 Toolkit v0.13.7

**Hover video controls** now has an on/off toggle directly in the toolbar menu, under **Browsing tools**.

Turn off the Toolkit’s seek and playback bar whenever you prefer. The choice applies immediately to open pages, is remembered across sessions and stays in sync with Settings. Turn it back on whenever you need it. Websites keep their own player controls.

Existing unified installations receive this signed update automatically. You can also use **Toolkit → Settings → Check for updates**, then close Toolkit tabs to activate it.
