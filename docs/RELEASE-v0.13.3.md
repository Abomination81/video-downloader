# Zillow and LoopNet, left alone.

**Abomination81 Toolkit v0.13.3** · Built by [abomination81](https://abomination81.com) · [X @abomination81](https://x.com/Abomination81)

Zillow and LoopNet are now whitelisted, including their subdomains. Ad filtering, cookie rejection, popup protection, hover video controls and copying/right-click overrides stay off on these sites so their listing and map interactions can work normally.

Manual video, audio and photo downloads, metadata cleaning and full-page screenshots remain available. Protection on other sites stays active. The toolbar identifies whitelisted sites, and the exceptions survive settings changes and Chrome restarts.

**Already using the unified extension?** The signed update installs automatically without new permissions or a reinstall. Refresh any open Zillow or LoopNet pages after it activates to clear filters already applied to those pages.
